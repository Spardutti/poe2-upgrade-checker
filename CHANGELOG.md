# Changelog

## 0.4.3 - 2026-10-23
- **Price checks you can trust.** *What's it worth?* used to take the cheapest listings at face value, so a few price-fixing baits at 1 ex, or one seller listing seven copies, set the price. Now it takes each seller's cheapest listing, leaves out baits far under the rest and listings over 14 days old, and gives the typical price with a range ("Worth about 1.2 div (most 1.1 – 1.3 div)"). It compares like with like: same rarity, same corrupted state, every stat at first and at most one missing after that, and always instant-buyout/in-person listings (your status setting is for upgrade searches). It reads 40 listings instead of 20 and updates as you load more. It says what makes the price uncertain – "only 2 listed – not enough to judge", "prices vary a lot", listings in a currency without a rate, a divine rate that's hours old or from another league – and won't give a number when too many listings can't be converted. The divine rate is fetched again when you switch league. Small prices read "0.3 ex", not "0 ex (0 div)".
- **Path of Building fixes:**
  - A Path of Exile 1 build, a cut-off export code or other text is refused with a clear message instead of loading an empty character that judged everything wrong.
  - Builds set to weapon set II are judged with the weapons you actually use.
  - Crossbows and quarterstaves are judged without your offhand.
  - If your main skill in PoB deals no damage, the app says so (pick your damage skill as the main skill) instead of showing ±0% damage; an item that gives you damage where you had none now counts it.
  - *Check an item* on Settings now gives the same Path of Building verdict as the popup.
  - Charms and flasks no longer show "Path of Building could not read this item" (they use the gear score), and PoB's own error is shown when it has one.
  - After Path of Building failed to start, importing a build, pressing Recalculate or turning PoB back on tries again straight away (it used to wait 10 minutes); a check right after the engine went idle no longer fails.
  - *Find upgrades* looks for the resistances you're short of against your real cap, not always 75%.
- **Popup fixes:** a late Path of Building answer for the previous item can't replace the one you're looking at; the popup's size follows the UI scale setting and never grows off the screen (it scrolls instead); *More ▾* and the search message stay as they were when the popup updates; "[Base]" no longer shows in stat names.
- **Smaller fixes:** a settings file that couldn't be read shows a warning at the top of the app instead of silently starting fresh; the item hotkey and the results-window hotkey can't be set to the same key; Ctrl/Alt + W/A/S/D no longer warns about movement keys; if loading listings fails, an open results window says so instead of showing the last search; the results help text explains LOSES RESISTANCE, LOSES DAMAGE and Path of Building verdicts; the Character page no longer skips an update that came in while it was drawing; the sign-in check isn't repeated on every settings change; "F2 was still held" now gets its own message on Linux.
- **From the review (settings, results, search, Linux):** new-character and delete dialogs that work with the keyboard, hotkey boxes you can Tab out of and Esc to undo, trade searches that pick local vs global mods by item type, no longer require an item's downsides (e.g. −Chaos Resistance), round price limits correctly and handle multi-line mods; gear scores count downsides on stats your build wants; the Linux AppImage has a fixed file name so it keeps updating and starting at login, and the F2 helper releases held Shift/AltGr first.
- **Character page tidied up:** everything is grouped into three columns – Offence, Defence, and Gear & build – instead of scattered cards.

## 0.4.2 - 2026-10-22
- **A new look: Wraeclast Iron.** The whole app – popup, trade results and every page – now looks like it belongs to Path of Exile 2: black and moonlit blue, carved capitals in parchment gold for names and headings, cast-iron frames with gold diamonds, verdicts struck like seals, resistance bars with a gold cap notch, and one blood-red button for the main action. Item names and mods keep the game's colours. The fonts (Cinzel, Alegreya Sans) are bundled, so it looks the same offline. The rules are in DESIGN.md.
- **Linux (experimental):** an AppImage for PoE2 under Steam/Proton (or Lutris/Heroic) on desktop Linux, X11 or Wayland. Everything works as on Windows – Path of Building verdicts (bundled LuaJIT), F2 with the same safeguards (the game is brought to the front, the popup moved out of the way), trade search, alerts, the Character page – and it updates itself. Ctrl+C is sent with X11's XTest through the system's Python, so there's nothing to install; without it the app uses xdotool, or you press Ctrl+C yourself. See the README's Linux section, and please report how it works on your setup.
- **Fixed: F2 often said "No item found".** Pressing F2 now works in the situations that broke it:
  - **after clicking one of the app's windows** (e.g. the results window over the game): the keystroke went to that window. The app now brings the game back to the front first – also when another app has focus but your mouse is over the game.
  - **with the last popup under the mouse:** the game saw the popup, not the item. The popup is now hidden before copying, and the mouse nudged 1 pixel so the game shows the item's tooltip again.
  - **right after the app started, or after its key helper restarted:** the press was dropped while the helper was starting. It now waits for it, and restarts it straight away if it stops.
  - **when the game is busy:** the app waits up to 1.5 s for the item text instead of half a second.
  - The automatic second Ctrl+C is gone (it never helped): one press, one Ctrl+C.
  - When nothing is copied, the popup says why – the game wasn't the active window (and which app was), the mouse wasn't over the game or was over the app's own window, the game runs as administrator, or no item was under the mouse – and the log says where the keystroke went.
- **Jewels are judged by Path of Building.** A jewel is tried in every jewel socket you've allocated on your PoB tree – replacing the jewel that's there, or in an empty socket – and the best one counts, so "vs Entropy Star (Socket #2)" tells you exactly where it goes. LOSES RESISTANCE / LOSES DAMAGE work for jewels too, and a jewel already in your tree is only compared with itself. *Find upgrades* on a jewel uses Path of Building's own jewel search (radius jewels search as radius jewels), and every listing is judged in your sockets. The Character page lists your jewel sockets and what's in them. Before, jewels only got the rough gear score.

## 0.4.1 - 2026-10-22
- **New Character page** (sidebar, under My gear): your whole character as Path of Building calculates it, with the gear you copied in game – class, ascendancy, level and passive points; minion DPS, hits, crit and their defences; your effective HP, energy shield (and recharge), armour, evasion, block and thresholds; resistances against the cap with bars; the biggest hit you can take of each type; Spirit reserved and free; attributes against requirements; movement speed and charges; every skill with its supports; keystones, ascendancy and notables; and your gear with gear scores. It updates when you change gear or switch character, and *Recalculate* reruns it. Without a PoB build it shows your resistances and gear and says how to get the full sheet.
- **Fixed: Find upgrades on My gear seemed to do nothing.** The search ran, but a minimised results window stayed in the taskbar, and the status message was at the bottom of the table. Now the results window is restored and brought to the front (moved onto your screen if it was left somewhere you can't see it), and the answer shows right under the button you clicked.
- **Never lose damage (new priority, on by default).** An item that costs more than 5% of your damage – e.g. a ring that caps lightning but doesn't have your minion damage mods – is now **LOSES DAMAGE** with a red banner ("Minion DPS 96,826 → 77,413"), however much resistance or effective HP it adds. Before, the resistance bonus could outweigh a −20% minion DPS loss and call it an UPGRADE. These items go to the bottom of every sort, and their score and value per divine aren't shown.
- **Better for my build keeps your damage mods.** Path of Building values filling a resistance very highly, so the search filled up with resistance-only items. Now every listing must also have as many of your build's damage mods as the item you wear (up to 2), and the resistances-first boost no longer outranks them. If nothing in your budget has them, the app says so and shows the best without that rule.
- **Clean-up pass before release.** Fixes that mattered:
  - The damage vs survivability slider on My gear was reversed (moving it toward survivability weighted damage more).
  - Priorities and the PoB status weren't shown correctly right after launch until you opened My gear.
  - Changing build or gear quickly could leave Path of Building judging with the old build; the newest one now always wins.
  - The popup could reappear by itself when a late Path of Building result came in after you closed it.
  - PoB import: mods with negative or fractional roll ranges, runes/enchants and jewels from an inactive tree were read wrong; talismans count as two-handed; mauls/shields detected properly.
  - Conditional mods ("while…", "if you've…", "per…") weigh less on your own item too, so it isn't unfairly beaten by listings.
  - Settings can't be lost to a half-written or locked file (retries, a backup copy, and the old file kept if it can't be read).
  - No more Windows error dialogs when the key sender or PoB engine stops unexpectedly; errors go to the log.
  - Trade results' gear score line is hidden when a Path of Building verdict is shown, so there's one clear answer per item.
- **Simpler:** results always open in the app window (setting removed); stat weights are only shown when your build is turned off; Priorities have their own card; the gear score is labelled as the fallback when PoB is on; each listing has one main action plus a small "on trade site ↗" link. "Options" is now "Settings" everywhere.
- **Chaos Inoculation is recognised** (life 1 in your PoB build): chaos resistance is then ignored everywhere – verdicts, warnings, the resistances-first search and the gear score – and My gear says so.
- **Resistances first in trade results.** The new default sort (*Resistances first*) puts items that cap a resistance you're short of at the top, then items that add toward the cap, and items that lose resistance at the bottom; the header counts how many cap one and how many lose one. Items that cap one get a green edge and "✓ Caps your … resistance".
- **Items that lose resistance can't be missed:** their own red **LOSES RESISTANCE** verdict, a red banner with exactly what drops (e.g. "Lightning 75% → 38%, −37 off the cap"), and a red card border – in the popup and in trade results, with or without Path of Building.
- **Priorities on My gear.** *Cap my resistances first* (on by default): while a resistance is under the cap, each point an item adds toward it counts +0.5% on its score (and each point lost below it costs the same), Find upgrades boosts the resistances you still need to the top of its search, and the gear score counts them double. Also: *including chaos*, *Never run short of Spirit* and *Keep attribute requirements met* – switch a check off and it no longer blocks an upgrade.
- **Fixed: Path of Building didn't start on Windows** ("attempt to index global 'arg'"): PoB expects the command line in `arg`, which the Windows host didn't set.
- **Fixed: price ranges were way off** (a 10 div minimum only showed items from ~27 div). The trade site's "Exalted Orb equivalent" converts divines with its own rate, far from the market's, so the app now asks for divine-priced listings in divines (and, for budgets up to 2 div, exalt-priced ones in exalts).
- **Path of Building engine: startup errors are shown** instead of the app waiting 90 seconds. If PoB can't start, the log and My gear say why, and the gear score is used meanwhile. A missing lua-utf8 library no longer stops it.
- **Works for any build.** The setup guide starts with your Path of Building export; damage is your Full DPS, your minions' DPS or your main skill's, whichever your build uses, and PoB's trade search raises that same number. Without PoB, importing a build picks the closest gear-score profile (minion summoner, ES caster or life + res) unless you chose one yourself; new installs default to life + res. The popup reminds you to add your PoB build when there isn't one.
- **Upgrades are now judged by Path of Building's own calculations.** Paste your PoB export on My gear and every item – in the popup and in trade results – is checked against your real build: the change to your minion DPS and effective HP, resistances against the cap, Spirit and attribute requirements. A swap that drops a resistance off the cap, leaves you short of Spirit or can't be worn is never a clean UPGRADE. Uniques get a verdict too.
- **Find upgrades uses Path of Building's trade search generator** (the same one as PoB's Trader): it tries every mod on your build, weights the search by what each one is worth to you, and every listing found is then checked exactly. Damage vs survivability is a slider on My gear.
- Items you copy in game replace PoB's in the reference build, so it calculates with what you're really wearing. If PoB's resistances disagree with your character sheet, My gear says so.
- The engine ships with the app (Path of Building 0.23.1, MIT licence); if it can't run, the gear score is used as before.
- **Fixed: "too many searches – wait 60 seconds" kept coming up.** Searches now pace themselves to the trade site's own limits (read from its replies) instead of getting the app banned for a minute. A rejected search is no longer sent a second time by another route, Better for my build remembers how big a search the site accepts so it stops wasting searches on ones it rejects, and background alerts step aside while you're searching.
- **Fixed: the Sign in window closed straight away and showed "Signed in" when you weren't.** pathofexile.com gives every visitor a session cookie, so the app now asks the site whether you're really signed in, and keeps the window open until you are.
- **Removed: stash value tracking**, until GGG releases a stash API for PoE2. Screen reading wasn't reliable enough, and public tabs only work as a workaround. F3 is free again.
- **Fixed: "Query is too complex" on Better for my build** (e.g. for a focus). When the trade site rejects a search for having too many filters, the app makes it smaller step by step instead of opening a failing page; signed-in searches (Options → Trade site account) go first, as the site allows bigger ones.
- Mageblood: **Legacy of …** lines are read and turned into their stats (e.g. Bismuth +45% all elemental resistances, Quicksilver 30% movement speed), with the duplicate-effect bonus. Ruby/Sapphire/Topaz (and other +max resistance) raise that element's cap. Re-copying an item you already wear doesn't shift your resistance totals.
- Resistances: totals now follow your gear (what you entered/imported, minus the gear you wore then, plus the gear you wear now), and there's a **safety buffer** (15% for PoB numbers, 5% for typed-in ones). Resistance keeps counting in the gear score until you're over the cap by the buffer, and a swap that leaves you only a little over the cap is marked **UPGRADE, BUT…** with a warning – PoB can count charms and buffs that aren't always on.
- Trade results: **Open this item on trade site** opens your browser on just that seller's listing (same search, narrowed to their account), so the site's own Travel to Hideout / whisper buttons are right at the top.
- Trade results: **Travel to hideout** now shows on every instant-buyout listing; the first time it asks you to sign in to pathofexile.com, then takes you there from the app.
- Trade results: a **Show 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 / 8 / all mods** picker for the stat changes on each card (remembered).

- **Better for my build finds real upgrades, not junk:** listings must have at least two of the slot's key stats at a decent roll (as many as your item has), conditional mods weigh less, and results come best-for-your-build first instead of cheapest first. The results header says how many are upgrades for you.
- **By my weights** is only offered when your build is turned off. It now refuses to search when none of your weights are on the item: before, it matched almost any jewel.
- **The gear score is explained in the app:** *What's the gear score?* in the trade results window, a *How the gear score works* card on Build & stats, and a link on My gear. Every listing's **why?** shows points per stat next to your item's, so you can see where a +9 comes from.
- **New layout that uses the whole screen.** A sidebar with the pages; side-by-side columns on a big or second monitor; it shrinks to a top bar in a small window. The main window reopens where you left it, including maximised on your other monitor.
- **Find upgrades has everything on one page:** item, price range, currency, sellers and league in one bar at the top, with ignored stats and saved searches next to it. The separate Search settings page is gone.
- **Stricter upgrade verdicts.** +6 gear score or more is an UPGRADE, +3 to +5 is a SMALL UPGRADE, and anything closer is a SIDEGRADE. "Better for my build" first looks for clearly better items.
- **UPGRADE, BUT…** warns when an item would cost Spirit (minions or auras might not fit), drop a resistance below the cap, or lose minion skill levels.
- Conditional mods ("…if you've Hit Recently", "while…") count for less in the gear score.
- Trade results flow into several columns when the results window is large.

- **Fixed: trade results showed "object Object" and every listing as a downgrade.** The trade site now sends item mods as objects; the app reads them again, so listings get their real stats and gear scores.
- **Find upgrades now looks for items that are better for your build** (higher gear score), even with a different mix of stats, instead of requiring every stat at your value or higher, which found almost nothing on good gear. The old search is still there: *Same stats or better* (popup → More, and the Find upgrade tab).
- **New: "If you equip it".** Every listing (and the popup) shows what changes on your character: resistances against the cap (with a warning if one drops below), Spirit, Energy Shield, Life, Mana, Intelligence, minion skill levels, movement speed and your overall gear score.
- **Fixed: Ctrl+C only worked once until you clicked the game.** The popup no longer takes keyboard focus from the game.
- **The hotkey moved to F2.** With WASD movement, Ctrl+D also walked your character and the copy failed. The app warns if you pick a movement or skill key.
- The hotkey now copies the item before anything appears on screen, so the game's tooltip doesn't vanish first.
- Implicit mods from advanced copies ({ Implicit Modifier }) are recognised, so searches use the right kind of stat.

- One-click releases: `release.bat` picks the next version, writes this changelog into the release notes, uploads, waits for the GitHub build and opens the release when the installer is live.
- New repo front page with a feature overview, download steps and a fair play section.
