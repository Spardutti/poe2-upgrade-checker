PoE2 Upgrade Checker

Hover an item in game and press Ctrl+D. A popup shows whether it beats what you have equipped. This window can be closed; the app keeps running in the system tray.

Import from Path of Building

Paste a PoB2 export code or a pobb.in link to load all your equipped gear at once (and your resistances). Your current gear for this character is replaced.

Your resistances (for resistance caps)

From your character sheet. Once you're capped (75%), more resistance on an item stops counting in the gear score. Leave empty to ignore caps.

Stash tabs

Open a specialty tab in game (Currency, Essence, Runes, Soul Cores, Omens, Delirium, Breach, Expedition, Abyss, Fragments, Idols) and press the scan hotkey. Each tab's value is kept until you scan it again. Rescanning shows what changed.

Your build

Gear score editor

How much each stat matters to you. 1× is the build's default, 0× turns it off, 2× doubles it. Changes apply to this character only.

Stat weights (advanced)

Only used for By my weights searches, and for the verdict when the build is turned off. A weight says how many points one unit of a stat is worth: Life 1 and Fire Res 1.5 means +10% fire res = +15 life.

Import / export weights

Paste item text here (from the game, or from someone's trade listing copied in game) to check it without the popup.

Hotkeys

Hover an item in game and press the check-item hotkey. The app presses Ctrl+C for you (one keystroke per press). Pick keys the game doesn't use, e.g. Ctrl+D, Alt+D or F2.

Trade site account (optional, experimental)

Sign in to pathofexile.com inside the app to use Travel to hideout on Instant Buyout listings straight from the results window. Your password goes only to pathofexile.com; the app keeps the login cookie on this PC.

Updates

The installed app checks GitHub for a new version when it starts and every few hours, downloads it quietly, and installs it the next time you quit or restart.

Troubleshooting

If the test popup appears here but not in game, switch PoE2 to Windowed Fullscreen (Options → Graphics). If the hotkey does nothing, open the log. Your data is saved in %APPDATA%\poe2-upgrade-checker.
